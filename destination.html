7 Destinations

Explore the Heart of India

From the sand-gold forts of Rajasthan to the emerald backwaters of Kerala — every corner of India is a story waiting to be lived.

Jaipur Goa Varanasi Kerala Rishikesh Leh-Ladakh Andaman
Rajasthan
Oct – Mar

Jaipur

The Pink City of Kings

Jaipur dazzles with amber-stone forts on rocky hilltops, maharaja palaces converted into luxury hotels, and bazaars overflowing with textiles, gems and blue pottery. The old walled city turns golden at dusk — one of India's most photogenic hours.

  • Top Attractions Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar
  • Must Eat Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, Pyaaz Kachori, Laal Maas
  • Known For Royal heritage, block-print textiles, camel safaris
Plan a Visit
Goa
Nov – Feb

Goa

The Pearl of the Orient

India's smallest state punches well above its weight — pristine beaches, Portuguese-era cathedrals, spice plantations and a laid-back coastal culture that is completely unlike anywhere else in the country. North Goa buzzes with energy; South Goa offers serenity.

  • Top Attractions Baga & Palolem beaches, Basilica of Bom Jesus, Dudhsagar Falls
  • Must Eat Fish Curry Rice, Prawn Balchão, Bebinca, Feni
  • Known For Beach parties, Portuguese churches, water sports
Plan a Visit
Uttar Pradesh
Oct – Mar

Varanasi

The City of Eternal Light

One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities, Varanasi sits on the western bank of the sacred Ganga. Its 88 ghats are alive around the clock — with pilgrims bathing at dawn, cremation pyres burning through the night, and the thunderous evening Ganga Aarti that draws thousands.

  • Top Attractions Dashashwamedh Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sarnath
  • Must Eat Kachori Sabzi, Banarasi Paan, Malaiyyo (winter), Lassi
  • Known For Spiritual rituals, silk weaving, sunrise boat rides
Plan a Visit
Kerala
Sep – Mar

Kerala

God's Own Country

A green ribbon of land between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea, Kerala offers a rare combination of calm backwaters, mist-wrapped tea estates, Ayurvedic wellness traditions and a coastline dotted with fishing villages. Drifting on a kettuvallam houseboat is one of India's most serene experiences.

  • Top Attractions Alleppey Backwaters, Munnar, Periyar, Kovalam Beach
  • Must Eat Appam with Stew, Kerala Sadya, Fish Molee, Banana Chips
  • Known For Houseboat stays, Ayurveda retreats, Kathakali dance
Plan a Visit
Uttarakhand
Sep – Apr

Rishikesh

The Yoga Capital of the World

Perched where the Ganga descends from the Himalayas into the plains, Rishikesh is simultaneously India's adventure-sports hub and a world-renowned centre for yoga and meditation. White-water rafting in the morning, evening aarti at dusk, and a silent ashram retreat — all within a few kilometres.

  • Top Attractions Lakshman Jhula, Parmarth Niketan, Beatles Ashram
  • Must Eat Aloo Puri, Chotiwala Thali, Rhino Café pancakes
  • Known For River rafting, yoga retreats, bungee jumping
Plan a Visit
Ladakh
May – Sep

Leh-Ladakh

Where the Sky Meets the Earth

At over 3,500 metres above sea level, Ladakh is India's high-altitude cold desert — a lunar landscape of rust-coloured mountains, turquoise lakes and ancient Tibetan-Buddhist monasteries clinging to cliffsides. The drive on the Manali–Leh Highway alone is one of the world's great road journeys.

  • Top Attractions Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, Hemis Monastery, Magnetic Hill
  • Must Eat Thukpa, Skyu, Momos, Butter Tea (Gur Gur Chai)
  • Known For Bike expeditions, monastery festivals, stargazing
Plan a Visit
Andaman & Nicobar
Oct – May

Andaman & Nicobar

India's Tropical Island Paradise

Six hundred kilometres off the eastern coast of mainland India, the Andaman Islands are a world apart — tropical rainforests, coral reefs teeming with marine life, bioluminescent beaches and a poignant colonial history told through the walls of the Cellular Jail. Radhanagar Beach consistently ranks among Asia's finest.

  • Top Attractions Radhanagar Beach, Cellular Jail, Neil Island, Barren Island
  • Must Eat Grilled Lobster, Coconut Prawn Curry, Fish Tikka, Crab Masala
  • Known For Scuba diving, snorkelling, sea kayaking, bioluminescence
